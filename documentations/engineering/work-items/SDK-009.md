### Context and evidence

### Maintainer notes

`LuaRegistrationSet` is the additive SDK transaction used by generated `TryRegisterLuaFunctions`. The legacy
`RegisterLuaFunctions`/`UnregisterLuaFunctions` pair remains for the Client 1.x package range and has not changed its
historical unconditional semantics. The new result returns a lease, collision policy, primary failure, independent
rollback report, and residual lease where compensation cannot be confirmed. The lease captures `(attachEpoch,
stateGeneration)` and exact rooted closures; it is idempotent and stale releases do not access Lua.

Executed locally: `dotnet restore CheatEngine.SDK.slnx`; focused native Lua registration tests; focused LuaBindings
generator tests; package-consumer tests; and the Windows x64 Native AOT publication probe. These prove managed,
fixture, source-generation, package-consumer, and AOT compilation behavior only. No Cheat Engine live proof was run.
